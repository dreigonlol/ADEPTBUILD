How to Remodel & Design Your Deck

Frequently Asked Questions

What should I consider when designing my deck?

Start with how you'll use the space—outdoor dining, entertaining, relaxing, or all three. Then factor in your yard's size, existing landscaping, and budget, since these shape the materials and features you can include. In Los Angeles, local building codes also affect deck size, height, and placement, so it's worth checking early. During a private consultation, we help you weigh each of these before deck construction begins.

How do I choose the size and shape of my deck?

Measure your available space and plan for furniture, traffic flow, and any extra features, then match the shape to your home's architecture. Common layouts include rectangular, square, L-shaped, and multi-level designs. Ground-level deck construction suits flat yards, while raised or multi-level decks work well on the sloped lots common across LA.

What materials are used for deck construction?

Three options lead most deck construction projects. Traditional wood—pressure-treated lumber, cedar, or redwood—offers a natural look but needs regular upkeep. Composite decking, a blend of wood fibers and recycled plastic, is durable, low-maintenance, and comes in many colors. PVC decking resists moisture, fading, and staining, ideal for humid or coastal areas. We'll help you choose the best composite decking or wood based on durability, aesthetics, and budget.

How can I get the most out of my deck?

Add features that match how you'll use the space. Built-in seating and storage benches save room and reduce clutter. An outdoor kitchen lets you cook and entertain without heading back inside. A fire pit or outdoor fireplace adds warmth and extends use into cooler months. Layered lighting—overhead fixtures, sconces, or pathway lights—keeps the deck safe and inviting after dark.

What are the latest deck design trends?

Today's most popular deck designs focus on usability, style, and sustainability. Multi-level decks define separate zones for dining and lounging. Integrated planters add greenery and privacy, while pergolas or shade structures create an outdoor living room feel. LED and low-voltage lighting illuminate the space, and low-maintenance materials like composite decking and aluminum railings continue to lead for their durability and eco-friendly appeal.

How long does it take to build or remodel a deck?

Smaller decks or simple remodels can take just a few days, while larger or multi-level projects may run several weeks. Site preparation, permitting, material delivery, and weather all affect the timeline. We set realistic expectations up front and keep you updated throughout the deck construction process so there are no surprises.

Do I need a permit to build a deck?

Usually, yes. Most Los Angeles decks over a certain size or height require a permit, along with any structural modifications. The process involves submitting plans, paying a fee, and getting approval from the local building department. As a licensed and insured contractor, we handle the permit process for you and keep your project fully code-compliant—saving you time and avoiding potential fines.

How do I maintain my deck for the long term?

Regular maintenance protects your investment. Clean the surface to remove dirt and mildew, inspect for loose boards or hardware, and sand, stain, or seal wood as needed. Keep the area around the deck clear of debris and vegetation to prevent moisture buildup and pests. We provide a detailed maintenance plan so your deck looks and performs its best for years to come.

Can I add a hot tub or outdoor kitchen to my deck?

Yes—with the right planning. A hot tub needs a sturdy foundation and may require reinforcing the deck structure to support the added weight. Outdoor kitchens need plumbing for sinks, gas lines for grills, and proper ventilation. We assess your deck's capacity, confirm compatibility, and coordinate the trades to ensure a safe, seamless installation.

How should I budget for my deck project?

Set an overall budget, then prioritize must-have features over nice-to-have upgrades. Costs cover materials, labor, permits, site preparation, and cleanup. We provide a detailed estimate based on your specific project and help you make informed decisions to stay on budget. When needed, we suggest cost-saving alternatives or a phased plan to fit your financial goals.
