About the Founder

Lior Ohayon is the founder and CEO of Adept Builders & Design, a premier construction and design-build firm serving discerning homeowners throughout Los Angeles. With more than a decade of experience in high-end residential construction, Lior is known for transforming ambitious visions into beautifully executed homes, renovations, and outdoor living spaces.

His hands-on leadership, meticulous attention to detail, and commitment to superior craftsmanship ensure every project is managed with precision from the initial consultation through final completion. Working closely with clients, architects, and designers, Lior delivers tailored spaces that reflect each homeowner’s lifestyle while adding enduring value to their property.

Lior is also the owner of NovaDeck, Adept Builders & Design’s sister company, specializing in innovative movable pool-deck solutions. This unique relationship gives Adept clients access to forward-thinking outdoor-living possibilities and integrated design solutions that are not typically available through a traditional construction company—all delivered with the same focus on quality, sophistication, and exceptional service.
