Adept Builders & Design

We are a Los Angeles based construction company that specializes in Home Additions, Full Home Renovation, Kitchen Remodeling, Roofing, Bathroom Remodeling, Pergolas, & Decks.

Adept Builders & Design is a fully licensed, bonded and insured contractor with more than a decade of experience in construction. We will meet objectives and create a clear plan to bring your vision to life, on time and within budget!

Using our industry knowledge, attention to detail and quality, we keep our clients coming back time and time again. We are here for you and take great pride in providing superb customer service!

Whether it is an upgrade to your home's interior or exterior, Adept Builders & Design has got you covered.
