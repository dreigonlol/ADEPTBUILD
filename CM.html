Design + Build

Rooted in architectural insight and built on over 25 years of hands on experience, Diversified Build delivers refined residential spaces with precision, integrity, and lasting value. We specialize in design-forward construction, seamlessly integrating architectural vision with masterful execution across new builds, additions, remodels. We have been working on new project.

From Beverly Hills to Malibu to Brentwood, we’ve completed luxury homes for discerning clients often returning to us for second and third residences. Our team includes seasoned project managers, in-house craftspeople, and over 90 trusted subcontractors, all aligned in delivering consistent, high-caliber results.

Kouros Etemad-trained at SCI-Arc and experienced alongside renowned architects such as Fredrick Fisher, we bring a deep understanding of systems, materials, and sequencing that elevates every project. Whether navigating complex hillside builds or delivering custom architectural details, we bring clarity, collaboration, and craftsmanship to every phase.

Why Choose Us?

25+ Years of Experience

We bring real-world knowledge and on-site expertise to every project.

Architectural Background from SCI-Arc

Kouros Etemad studied architecture at the Southern California Institute of Architecture (SCI-Arc) and had the opportunity to work with renowned architect Fredrick Fisher.

Mastery of Detail

With a background in architecture, we deliver precision in materials, systems, and finish-level details.

Proven Success on Challenging Sites

We have a strong track record of building homes and pools on hillside and technically demanding lots.

Transparent & Collaborative Process

We work closely with clients, architects, and project teams from start to finish to ensure clarity, efficiency, and quality.

Trusted In-House Team

With 20 dedicated employees and over 50 reliable subcontractors, we ensure consistent performance and seamless project execution.

Client Relationships

At Diversified Build, many of our clients return to build with us again. These long-term relationships are a direct result of the trust, quality, and satisfaction we deliver project after project.

What We Do
